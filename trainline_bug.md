## [Windows 11] "Find destinations by budget" opens a blank page
## Device: 
Desktop

## Steps to reproduce:
Preconditions:
The user has a valid Trainline account.
The user is logged in to the Trainline website.

1. Open https://www.thetrainline.com/en-us.<br>
2. Open the hamburger menu next to the username.<br>
3. Click “Find destinations by budget”.

## Expected result:
The "Find destinations by budget" page opens successfully and displays its content.

## Actual result:
A blank page is displayed after clicking “Find destinations by budget”.

## Type:
Functional

## Severity:
High
