# [Android 16] Show descriptions are truncated and cannot be scrolled in Shows and Parades <br>
Device: Xiaomi Redmi 15

## Steps to reproduce:
1.Open the Disneyland Paris application.<br>
2.Navigate to Shows and Parades.<br>
3.Open Indoor Stage Shows – Guaranteed Access.<br>
4.Tap the circular “i” information icon next to a show title.<br>
5.Scroll down inside the show information screen.<br>
6.Repeat the same action for other available shows.<br>

## Expected result:
The complete show information is displayed, and the user can scroll through the content to read the entire description, including the text at the bottom of the page.

## Actual result:
The show description is cut off before the end. The user cannot scroll the information view, so the remaining text and lower content cannot be accessed.

## Type: 
Visual

## Severity: 
Low
